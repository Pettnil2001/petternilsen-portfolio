I'm a cybernetics and robotics engineer who just finished an M.Sc. at NTNU. These days I split my time between a research assistant role at NTNU — supporting teaching and labs in control systems and guidance, navigation and control — and a systems engineer role at PQtech, designing PCBs and measurement electronics and working through multi-terabyte GPS, IMU, and power datasets.

Before that, I spent two years at Equanostic building a pipe-inspection robot for my master's thesis — PCB design, motor control, and an inertial navigation solution combining IMU, encoder, and computer-vision measurements. And before that, a bachelor's in Electrical Engineering (automation and robotics) at NTNU, a semester on exchange at the University of Queensland, and hands-on experience everywhere from a drone team to a wildfire crew to a student-run rural electrification project.
