I'm a master's student in Cybernetics and Robotics at NTNU, currently splitting my time between coursework and a part-time role building an inspection robot at Equanostic. I like the parts of engineering where hardware and software have to agree with each other — laying out a PCB, tuning a motor control loop, or getting a sensor fusion pipeline to actually trust its own numbers.

Before this I finished a bachelor's in Electrical Engineering (automation and robotics) at NTNU, spent a semester on exchange at the University of Queensland, and picked up hands-on experience everywhere from a drone team to a wildfire crew to a student-run rural electrification project.
